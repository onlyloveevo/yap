# From the Loom to the build and roadmap

[Watch the original YAP demo](https://www.loom.com/share/fa69dbc3d8f2413a80f135eaf3a472c2). Times below are approximate, taken from its transcript. The video mixes the product idea, a demonstration and future plans; a spoken claim alone is not evidence that a feature works in this package.

**Snapshot reviewed:** `d06535f6e12eee0f0eb623b504ed6bddb38ac0c9` (4 October 2026, 22:37). On 4 October, AI judges that had built none of it walked the 25 functions the demo shows (L1 to L25) by doing each one on the page, with real speech audio and a camera feed. 22 worked and 3 worked in part. They judged the 21:53 build; this one adds the small repairs made after it. No outside person has walked it yet. See [README](../README.md) for the check scope and [ROADMAP](../ROADMAP.md) for the original four-week targets.
